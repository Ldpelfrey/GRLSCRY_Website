Every tee is made to order — nothing is printed until you buy it. Production plus delivery takes 2–3 weeks from the day your order is placed. You will get a confirmation email at checkout; tracking follows once the order leaves the printer.

Because each shirt is printed to order, we do not accept returns or exchanges for size or change of mind — please use the size guidance before ordering.
